Moroccan Dama Rules (الضامة)

  • 8×8 Street Board: Played on the 32 dark squares—Bottle Caps (Bouchonat) move first against Street Rocks (7ejrat).
  • Men (Pawns): Move 1 square diagonally forward. Capture diagonally forward only (never backward).
  • Flying Kings (Dama): Reaching the far row flips your bottle cap or stacks your rock into a Dama—moving and capturing at any open diagonal distance in all 4 directions.
  • Mandatory Maximum Capture: If a capture exists, you must capture—and must choose a path capturing the greatest total number of pieces.
  • Equal Captures: Equal-length maximum captures are freely selectable (no king tie-break in initial profile).
  • Crowning Mid-Jump: Reaching the far row crowns a man immediately and ends the turn.
